[h1]Instant Pick Block[/h1]
[b]Press F once to pick the structure under your cursor — Minecraft-style.[/b]

Vanilla pick block needs you to hold **Picker**, click, then release. This mod picks on one key press.

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Aim the cursor at a structure in the world.
[*]Press [b]F[/b] (or your bound [b]Picker[/b] key).
[*]The structure type is ready to place from the hotbar, with filter and color copied when vanilla would copy them.
[/olist]

Rebind under [b]Options → Controls → Pick Block[/b].

[h2]Options[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Mod enabled[/b] — turn off to restore vanilla Picker (hold F, click, release) on the next game restart or hot reload.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Empty cells and blocked structures show the same toasts as vanilla.
[*]The shortcut helper may still say “Hold F”; the key now picks on press.
[/list]
