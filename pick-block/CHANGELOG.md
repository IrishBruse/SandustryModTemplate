# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Changed: updated for **Sandustry 0.5.5**. Requires game **0.5.5** or newer.

## 1.0.1

- Changed: pressing **Picker** over empty ground no longer starts placing.

## 1.0.0

- Added: press **Picker** (default **F**) once to pick the structure under the cursor. No hold-and-click.
- Added: **Mod enabled** in Options → Mods. Turn off to use vanilla hold-to-pick again.
