# Minecarts

Bulk cargo line: **rail**, **loader**, and **unloader**. Drag rail diagonally to place an incline. Carts lift powder out of the falling-sand grid while they travel, so a long run costs less sim work than a belt highway.

Carts stay level. They do not drive up walls. A diagonal drag makes an incline.

## Use

1. Enable the mod and load a save.
2. Build **Minecart Rail** from **Minecarts** in the building menu. Drag sideways for flat track. Drag **diagonally** for an incline. The cart stays upright.
3. Place a **Loader** above the rail. Dump powder onto the hopper. The cart on the rail below fills from that pile.
4. Place an **Unloader** under the rail, above your factory belts. The cart dumps through the pad onto the belts.
5. Select the **Minecart** tool (Construction). Click a rail to place a cart. Demolish (**X**) a cart to remove it. Demolish the rail to drop the cart.

Carts reverse at a dead end. They wait when the next cell is occupied. They wait on a loader until the pad is empty or the cart is full (64 cells). They wait on an unloader until cargo is gone.

## Controls

- **Minecart tool** — click a rail to place a cart.
- **X** (demolish) — click a cart to remove it. The rail stays.

## Options

- **Mod enabled** — turn rails, stations, and carts off.

## Notes

- Cargo is a compact count. It does not fall out on a ramp.
