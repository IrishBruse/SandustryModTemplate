[h1]Pixel-perfect Screenshot and GIF recorder[/h1]
[b]Pixel-perfect PNG and GIF of your C selection.[/b]

Share a machine, a line, or a whole scene without a blurry screenshot. Screenshot copies a PNG to the clipboard. Record GIF steps the sim and downloads an animation. Optional greenscreen for chroma key.

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Press [b]C[/b] and drag a box around the area.
[*]Keep that box on screen.
[*]Press [b]F7[/b] to open the panel.
[*][b]Screenshot[/b] copies a PNG. Paste with Ctrl+V.
[*][b]Record GIF[/b] downloads an animated GIF of sim ticks.
[/olist]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]2× nearest-neighbor — sharp pixels, no blur
[*]GIF of stepped sim ticks (2–120 frames, 1–30 ticks per frame)
[*]Greenscreen (#00FF00) for chroma key
[*]HUD and marquee handles stay out of the image
[*]Record GIF clears the C selection when it starts so you can keep playing during the capture
[/list]

[h2]Limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]The crop is the selection box, plus 1 px on each edge
[*]A selection that is off-screen cannot be captured — pan the camera and try again
[/list]
