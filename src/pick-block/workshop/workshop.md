# Instant Pick Block

**Press F once to pick the structure under your cursor — Minecraft-style.**

Vanilla pick block needs you to hold **Picker**, click, then release. This mod picks on one key press.

## How to use

1. Aim the cursor at a structure in the world.
2. Press **F** (or your bound **Picker** key).
3. The structure type is ready to place from the hotbar, with filter and color copied when vanilla would copy them.

Rebind under **Options → Controls → Pick Block**.

## Options

- **Mod enabled** — turn off to restore vanilla Picker (hold F, click, release) on the next game restart or hot reload.

## Notes

- Empty cells and blocked structures show the same toasts as vanilla.
- The shortcut helper may still say “Hold F”; the key now picks on press.
